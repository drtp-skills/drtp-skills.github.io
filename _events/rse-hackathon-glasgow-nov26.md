**Open hackathon to prototype discipline-overarching Research Software Engineering (RSE) frameworks.**

RSE practice and training remain fragmented along disciplinary lines, with each field reinventing tooling, conventions, and teaching material. We are running an open hackathon to prototype discipline-overarching RSE frameworks — shared, reusable building blocks that work across the social sciences, life sciences, and the humanities rather than within a single silo.

In two days, the team will produce an overall system design (such as a UML diagram) and a working end-to-end prototype. We will use a thin-slice approach: rather than spending time building a complete database or UI, we will connect just enough of each layer to show a user interacting with a real, working feature.

*Practical information*

The two-day hackathon will take place at University of Glasgow, Scotland on the 26th and 27th of November 2026:

- Day 1: lightning talks framing the challenges, team formation, and prototyping
- Day 2: build a short showcase and a community roadmap for carrying winning prototypes forward.

Hybrid attendance for remote contributors is also possible.

The coolest module gets an award and stipend.
