At their next monthly meetup, the **RCM Cooperative** will be making space to pause, reflect and learn together.

- 📅 **Tuesday 15 December 2026 at 1pm to 2pm UK time/UTC**
- 🔗 **[Registration link](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/mBcJAu_sRa6IG0giYO-U_w#/registration)**

The session theme is Community Reflections 2026, and the team will be exploring the experiences, challenges and learning that have shaped their community-building work over the past year.

Guiding the conversation will be:
- ✨ Dr. Eirini Zormpa, Scientific Community Manager, Imperial College London
- ✨ Dr. Cassandra Gould van Praag, Co-Executive Director, RCM Cooperative

The meetup is for everyone involved in Research Community Management, whether it’s your formal role, one part of your work, or an area you’d like to learn more about.

Come and reflect on 2026 with the community, make new connections and share what you’d like to carry into the year ahead. 🌱

**[RCM Cooperative](https://www.rcmcooperative.com/)** is a member-led cooperative working to professionalise Research Community Management and help research communities become more equitable, resilient and collaborative.
