Date and time: Wednesday 21 October 2026, 12:00-13:00 BST

The RCM Cooperative monthly meetups are a place for those working in formal and informal research community management roles, or just interested in research community work, to gather together to network, exchange knowledge and discuss relevant topics such as community mapping, engagement strategies and communications. We will hear talks from our community of RCMs sharing their knowledge and real-world experience.

This month’s theme: Community and RCM narratives

This month’s speaker:

- Michael Donnay, Community Manager, Institute for Research Software

RCM Cooperative is a member-led cooperative that supports the professionalisation of Research Community Managers and helps research communities become more equitable, resilient, and collaborative.
